RETURNS & WARRANTY
What can be claimed — pick an order or a completed service to start a request.
Replacement or back job
We put it right — the same part again, or the mechanic redoes the work — at no charge.
Replacement Back job No money returned
Refund
We return what you paid. The part isn't replaced and the work isn't redone — book again if you still need it.
Money back Cash or GCash
Parts can be returned within 7 days of delivery or pickup Services have a 15-day warranty from the day the job was done
Replacement limit: 2 requests per item
If a part is defective, you can ask for a replacement up to 2 times. Only approved replacements count — if the shop declines a request (for example, because the damage came from misuse or improper installation), it doesn't use up one of your chances. If the same item is defective a third time after 2 approved replacements, you won't be able to submit a request online anymore — you and the shop will need to talk it over and agree on what to do next.
