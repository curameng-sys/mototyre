What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. MotoTyres North Caloocan uses them to keep you signed in, keep your account secure, and remember your settings.

Payments

When you pay with GCash, you're taken to PayMongo's secure checkout. PayMongo may set its own cookies there to process your payment, and its privacy policy applies to them.

Map

The map in our Contact section is from Google Maps. Google may set its own cookies when the map loads, and its privacy policy applies to them.

Changing your choice

Click at the bottom of any page to turn analytics cookies on or off. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings, but you'll be signed out.

Your privacy rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you can ask what personal data we keep about you and ask us to correct or delete it. Call or text us at 0915 269 8366, or message MotoTyres North Caloocan on Facebook.
